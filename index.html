Storage that fits your order

Our storage sizes change with the quantity a supplier requests. A small trial order and a full truckload are allocated different space, and we confirm the space and conditions with you before the first shipment arrives.

Climate-controlled storage

Temperature-controlled space for products that are sensitive to heat or cold, such as supplements, beauty products and electronics.

Ambient storage

Standard dry storage for products with no special temperature requirements, such as many packaged goods and accessories.
